## 1. Executive Summary

The Adani Group, through its subsidiary Endeavour Trade and Investment Ltd, has agreed to acquire Holcim Group's entire 63.15% controlling stake in Ambuja Cements Ltd — India's second-largest cement manufacturer — for ₹385 per share. The transaction includes indirect control of ACC Ltd through Ambuja's 50.05% holding, along with mandatory open offers for both companies. At a total outlay of approximately ₹81,361 crores (~US$10.5 billion), this represents the largest-ever M&A transaction in India's cement sector and marks the Adani Group's entry into the building materials industry.

The deal is strategically compelling on three dimensions: (i) it immediately positions Adani as India's second-largest cement producer with ~67.5 MTPA capacity, providing a platform for further consolidation; (ii) the acquisition generates substantial run-rate synergies of ₹3,100 crores through procurement leverage, logistics optimization, and operational efficiencies across the combined 67.5 MTPA capacity base; and (iii) it provides a natural hedge to Adani's infrastructure portfolio by vertically integrating a key construction input material.

## 2. Strategic Rationale

### 2.1 Immediate Scale & Market Position

India's cement industry is highly fragmented, with the top five players controlling approximately 45% of total installed capacity (~570 MTPA in FY2022). Through this acquisition, Adani instantly gains:

- **67.5 MTPA combined capacity** (Ambuja: 31.45 MTPA + ACC: 36.04 MTPA), establishing a #2 position behind UltraTech Cement (~117 MTPA)
- **Pan-India presence** across 12 integrated plants, 13 grinding stations, and 6 bulk terminals spanning North, West, East, and Central India
- **Premium brands** — "Ambuja" and "ACC" are among India's most recognized cement brands with 80+ years of combined heritage

### 2.2 Vertical Integration with Infrastructure Portfolio

The Adani Group's existing portfolio — spanning ports, airports, roads, power generation, and transmission — represents one of India's largest consumers of cement and construction materials. This acquisition creates a powerful vertical integration thesis:

- **Captive demand absorption**: Adani's infrastructure projects consume an estimated 8–10 MTPA of cement annually, providing a built-in demand floor
- **Logistic synergies**: Adani Ports & SEZ operates India's largest private port network; cement dispatch via coastal shipping reduces per-tonne freight costs by ₹200–350 versus rail/road
- **Counter-cyclical hedge**: Cement demand is positively correlated with infrastructure capex cycles, providing earnings stability during commodity downturns affecting the group's resources businesses

### 2.3 India Macro Tailwinds

India's cement demand is projected to grow at 6–8% CAGR through 2030, driven by:

- **Government infrastructure spending**: National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) targets ₹111 lakh crore investment by FY2025; PM Gati Shakti and National Logistics Policy further accelerate execution
- **Housing for All**: Continued demand from affordable housing under PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana)
- **Urbanization**: India's urban population is expected to reach 600 million by 2030, driving housing and commercial real estate demand

The demand–supply dynamics favor price discipline, with utilization rates expected to improve from ~65% to ~75% over the medium term.

The premiums paid are **below the median** of comparable Indian cement M&A transactions (median: ~15.2%), reflecting Holcim's strategic decision to exit India entirely as part of its global portfolio simplification strategy. The below-median premium is justified by:

- Holcim's publicly stated intention to divest, reducing its bargaining power
- Adani being one of very few credible acquirers capable of a $10.5B transaction in India
- No formal auction process — bilateral negotiation favored the buyer

At 14.3x FY2022 EV/EBITDA, the implied valuation is supported by:

- **DCF cross-check**: WACC of 11.0% and 4.5% terminal growth implies a fair value range of 13.5x–15.8x forward EBITDA
- **Replacement cost**: building 67.5 MTPA greenfield capacity would cost an estimated ₹1,00,000+ crores (at $180/tonne) and take 5–7 years, versus paying ₹81,361 crores for operational assets
- **Forward multiple compression**: at projected FY2025E EBITDA of ₹8,398 crores (with synergies), the implied EV/EBITDA drops to ~10.2x, well below the sector average

### 4.2 Financing Rationale

The **35% equity / 50% debt / 15% target cash** mix reflects a balanced approach:

- **Equity commitment of ₹20,000 crores** demonstrates promoter confidence and maintains skin-in-the-game, critical for lender comfort and rating agency assessment
- **Weighted average cost of debt of ~8.75%** is achievable given the asset quality, combined EBITDA generation of ₹5,352 crores, and Adani Group's established banking relationships

Post-close, the combined entity generates robust cash flows to service debt:

- **Interest coverage ratio: ~2.1x** in Year 1, improving to **~3.5x** by Year 3 as synergies ramp and debt is repaid
- **No dividend leakage**: dividends from target entities flow up to service acquisition debt
- Combined **free cash flow yield of ~6.5%** supports rapid deleveraging

## 7. Conclusion & Recommendation

The Adani Group's acquisition of Ambuja Cements and ACC Ltd represents a **transformative, strategically sound transaction** that:

1. **Creates immediate scale** — catapulting Adani to #2 position in India's cement sector with 67.5 MTPA
2. **Generates substantial synergies** — ₹3,100 crores run-rate with clear, executable levers in procurement, logistics, and SG&A
3. **Achieves attractive pricing** — 13.2% premium is below precedent median; EV/tonne of $160 is at a significant discount to greenfield replacement cost
4. **Becomes EPS accretive** — by FY2024E (+8.4%), with a manageable deleveraging trajectory
5. **Provides strategic optionality** — platform for further sector consolidation and vertical integration with Adani's infrastructure ecosystem

The financing mix is appropriately structured, with promoter equity commitment providing credit confidence. While the initial leverage is elevated, the robust combined cash flow generation and identified synergy pipeline provide a credible path to investment-grade credit metrics within 3 years.

**We recommend proceeding with the transaction as structured.**

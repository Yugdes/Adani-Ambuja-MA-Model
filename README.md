# Merger Consequences Model — Adani Group / Ambuja Cements & ACC

[![Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/View-Dashboard-06b6d4?style=for-the-badge)](https://Yugdes.github.io/Adani-Ambuja-MA-Model/dashboard/index.html)

Merger consequences analysis of the Adani Group's 2022 acquisition of Holcim's Indian cement business (Ambuja Cements and ACC). The model is built on filed financials and published deal terms. It covers purchase price allocation, look-through goodwill, minority interests, holdco financing, synergy value and returns.

## Conclusion

**The premium is covered by synergies, but earnings returns trail the cost of capital and the holdco cannot service its debt from dividends.**

- Adani's share of the net synergy NPV is ₹10,701 Cr against ₹3,992 Cr of premium and transaction costs, creating ₹6,709 Cr of value. Breakeven run-rate synergies are ₹1,310 Cr versus ₹3,100 Cr modelled.
- Cash ROIC on the ₹51,653 Cr invested reaches 6.7% by FY2025E, below the 12.78% unlevered cost of capital; return on sponsor equity is 1.4% against a 25.8% levered cost of equity. The price is 14.0x CY2021 EBITDA, so near-term earnings yields are low.
- Dividends received cover 0.12x of holdco interest in FY2025E; the shortfall of ₹2,365 Cr a year must come from refinancing, asset sales or sponsor equity.
- At US$161/tonne, the deal is 40% above the median of 5 non-distressed Indian cement precedents (US$115/t), consistent with buying a pan-India, net-cash, two-brand platform rather than single assets.

## Methodology

1. **Deal and ownership.** Holcim's 63.11% of Ambuja and 4.48% of ACC, plus open-offer shares actually tendered. The resulting stakes tie to Adani's reported 63.15% / 56.69%.
2. **Financing.** Reported US$ acquisition debt sits at the offshore holdco, with sponsor equity as the balancing source. Target cash is not used.
3. **PPA and goodwill.** Look-through: consideration is split between Ambuja ex-ACC and ACC at the offer prices, and goodwill is consideration less Adani's share of fair-value net assets (Ind AS 103 proportionate NCI).
4. **Projections.** CY2021 audited standalone base with assumed growth and margins. ACC dividends are removed from Ambuja's income as intra-group.
5. **Pro forma.** Consolidated (100%) earnings, attributed to Adani and non-controlling interests. Holdco interest gets no tax shield (offshore SPV), and the holdco cash schedule shows dividends against interest.
6. **Returns.** Return on sponsor equity vs relevered cost of equity, cash ROIC vs unlevered cost of capital, and a value test of Adani's share of synergy NPV vs premium paid plus costs.
7. **Sensitivities.** Every cell is a full re-run of the model: offer price × synergies, refinancing rate, EBITDA margin and tax deductibility.
